# Campus Mobile 2026 — Regulation Fit

**Source:** Regulamento da 15ª edição do Programa Campus Mobile.

## Eligibility fit

Current known fit:
- user is currently enrolled in a Brazilian undergraduate program;
- age/residency/author requirements must remain satisfied at submission;
- participation may be individual or in a team of up to 3 people;
- only one project/category may be submitted per participant/team.

## Project maturity

The program accepts projects in:
- **specification**, meaning a detailed idea with context, target audience, and innovative aspects; or
- **implementation**, meaning technical solution search and/or partial prototypes have begun.

This is compatible with our current stage.

## Mandatory constraint

The proposal must **obligatorily involve mobile telephony**, using phones or other platforms that use mobile networks.

This is the main reason a separate variant exists.

## Working category

**Tecnologias Urbanas** is the current best-fit category because the regulation explicitly includes:
- B2B and B2C products/services;
- workplace improvement;
- productivity;
- automation;
- support for companies and economic activities.

The organizer may recategorize a project if another category is considered more appropriate.

## Application dates

- applications: 14/08/2026 to **18/10/2026 at 23:59 Brasília time**;
- selected projects announced: 04/12/2026;
- preparatory phase: 18/01/2027 to 19/02/2027;
- immersive week in São Paulo: 21/02/2027 to 26/02/2027.

## Application format

The application form has:
1. candidate general data;
2. candidate profile;
3. project / mobile service idea.

An optional video of up to 2 minutes may be submitted and is encouraged by the organizer.

## Awards / progression

The regulation includes:
- R$ 2,700 net per project for the top 3 projects in each category after the immersive stage;
- R$ 9,000 net for Stage 4 projects that meet their goals;
- the best project in each category may receive the San Francisco / Silicon Valley immersion trip.

For the final trip, the program covers national/international transportation, accommodation, food, and programmed activities as specified by the regulation.

## Important note

This file is a working eligibility summary, not a substitute for rereading the regulation before submission.
